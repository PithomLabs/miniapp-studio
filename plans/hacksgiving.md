So what am I building?

A personal agent that lives on a Sprite: a hardware isolated Linux computer that is exactly as persistent or disposable as you want it to be. Your agent should also use at least one Sprites connector. (Might we suggest one from our lovely partners above? 😉)

    Runs on a Sprite
    Uses at least one Sprites connector

Other than those two elements, the world is your oyster (sorry, we’re getting hungry).
Uh, how do I hack and eat dinner at the same time?

Great news. Your hackathon compute is on us with Fly’s new Free plan. And every Sprite comes with Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Antigravity, Cursor, Pi and Grok already installed. Bring your own model subscription and start building.

Here’s how the hackathon works.

    1 Sign up for Fly.io (or ask your coding agent to do it for you).
    2 Build an agent that runs on Sprites and uses at least one Sprites connector.
    3 Share it and submit your link here by midnight PST on Sunday, November 29.
    4 We’ll announce the winners on Tuesday, December 1 by email.

How to submit

    1 Build. Build an agent on Sprites during Hacksgiving week, and make sure it uses at least one Sprites connector.
    2 Share. Post your agent on X, Bluesky, LinkedIn, or Reddit with a short demo, and tag us @flydotio. (Video demos always earn “bonus points,” but won’t actually affect your score.)
    3 Submit. Send us a link to your code and your demo, along with a sentence or two about what your agent does, before midnight PST on Sunday, November 29.

Rules and guidelines

    Hacksgiving runs from Monday, November 23 through Sunday, November 29. Submissions close before midnight PST on November 29.
    Your project must be built during Hacksgiving week.
    Your project must be an agent that runs on Fly.io Sprites using at least one connector.
    You need a Fly.io account to enter. Create it yourself or ask a coding agent to do it for you.
    Use any AI tools you like to help you build.
    Each submission needs a link to your code, a demo, and a short description of what your agent does.
    Developers everywhere are welcome to submit 1 entry.
    Projects have to follow the Fly.io terms of service.
    Our judges will pick the winners of all three prizes, and we will announce them on Tuesday, December 1st 2026.
